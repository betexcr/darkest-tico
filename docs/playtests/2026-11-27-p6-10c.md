# P6-10c — Bosque Nuboso Maldito against the Sótanos

The Sótanos are tuned to the owner's per-tier bands (Aprendiz 85–90, Veterano 70–80, Campeón 55–70% cleared;
P5-12, long campaigns). The Bosque opens only after the Mayordomo, so the affordable, comparable check is **the
same bot runs on both regions**; the target is the Bosque within ±5 points of the Sótanos on each tier.

## La Segua (boss sim, 2000 fights, party at the region's level: Bosque 1/4/6, Sótanos 0/3/5, ranks 2/3/4, gear above Aprendiz)

| Setting | Aprendiz | Veterano | Campeón |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mayordomo (Sótanos), for reference | — | 99.8% | 98.5% |
| P6-10a: two mirror images at 60% PV (+1 at 30% from Veterano) | 99.4% | 88.0% | 45.5% |
| Campeón Galope: stress 5→3, susto 25→15% | — | 88.0% | 48.2% |
| … and weight 25→15 | — | 88.0% | 48.6% |
| … stress 2, susto 10%, damage 2–5 | — | 88.0% | 48.5% |
| Veterano phase without Chiva | — | 86.8% | 45.5% |
| No Veterano phase (no image at 30%) | — | 93.2% | 66.2% |
| Images at 60% hit for 0 stress | — | 87.9% | 44.3% |
| **One image at 60% (+1 at 30% from Veterano) — kept** | 99%+ | **97.2%** | **83.7%** |
| One image at 60%, no Veterano phase | — | 99.4% | 94.8% |

The number of mirror images was the lever: every extra image is another body the party has to find among
illusions, and Campeón stress came from the length of the fight, not from Galope de niebla.

What this showed:

- **Veterano and Campeón are on par with the Sótanos** once La Segua makes one image at 60% instead of two
  (Campeón 74% against 72%; Veterano 6 points under, consistent with a region one level higher).
- **Fresh level-0 parties spiral in the Bosque** (one campaign in three flees La Segua with stress near 170 in
  week 1 and carries the stress into every later week), but no fresh party can enter it: it opens after the
  Mayordomo. Parties at the level the Sótanos leave them clear 100% (Sótanos 96%), so Aprendiz may be a little
  easy for them; the owner's playtest decides.
- Deaths: 2 in 384 Bosque expeditions (both Campeón).
