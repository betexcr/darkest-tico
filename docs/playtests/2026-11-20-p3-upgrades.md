What this showed:

- **The order a player buys in matters more than the prices.** v1's bot spent each new Reliquia on an upgrade
  before skill ranks; rank spending fell from 1528 to 387 ₡ a week and fresh parties stalled (Aprendiz 57%).
  Ranks and gear first, upgrades with what is left (v2), put every number back.
- **The rank cap holds Campeón back unless Maestría is bought.** v2 rarely had money left for it; Campeón
  parties stayed at rank 3 (46%, 0.46 deaths there). Buying Maestría when a hero is capped, and Plan de
  Formación, before ranks (v3) fixed the deaths (0.18 at Campeón).
- **Colones, not Reliquias, were the limit** (v3: 20 Reliquias unspent, 5 of 24 levels). Halving the colones
  prices (v4) makes Reliquias the limit: 15 of 24 levels by week 24, 10 Reliquias unspent, every campaign
  solvent, money in = out.
- **Open: the tiers moved.** With upgrades (cheaper, stronger relief; rank 4 at Veterano) Veterano clears 86%
  (band 70–80), and more campaigns reach Campeón (39 expeditions, was ~20) where rank-3/4 parties clear 49%
  (band 55–70; deaths there 0.33). Enemy numbers were left alone in this task; the retune is P5-11.
