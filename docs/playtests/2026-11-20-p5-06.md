What this showed:

- **Drops didn't move the tiers.** Veterano 74% and Campeón 60% on the old bot, inside the bands (70–80 / 55–70).
  Campeón is only ~20 expeditions per setting (5–6 of 18 campaigns get there in 24 weeks), so 60% vs 74% is noise;
  P5-08's tier-start runs (63%) remain the better Campeón number.
- **A bot report bug hid the Iglesia.** Iglesia/Cantina weeks booked before the party was picked were reported as
  "Hires" (~700 ₡/week; recruits are free). Fixed: Iglesia/Cantina relief is 1215 ₡/week, about 27% of spending,
  the second sink after skill ranks.
- **Broke campaigns spiralled.** With no money for relief, heroes at 150+ stress went out anyway and the bot's
  retreat rule (fight dragging, someone at 150+) pulled them out of the first fight, every week, with no loot
  (seed 5, campaign 3: weeks 19–24 at 5–356 ₡). The bot now leaves them home to rest (free idle relief) and sends
  free recruits. Deaths 0.13 → 0.11, weeks under 500 ₡ 19 → 9.
- **The bank band is not an enemy-strength problem.** Money in ≈ out every week; the bot spends down to its 1000 ₡
  reserve (ranks, relief, gear), so the week-24 bank mostly shows where a campaign is in its spending cycle
  (169 to 13.5k). With rewards frozen (owner 2026-11-15), the levers left are prices (Iglesia/Cantina, ranks) or
  the bot's reserve, not enemy numbers.
