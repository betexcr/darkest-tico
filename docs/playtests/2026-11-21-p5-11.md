What this showed:

- **Six seeds are not enough.** The unchanged data gave Veterano 78% / Campeón 67% on seeds 1–6 (inside both
  bands) and 86% / 81% on seeds 7–12. Every setting from v2 on uses 12 seeds (36 campaigns, so ≥ 60 Campeón
  expeditions match the plan's ≥ 30 per 18).
- **With the Feria and upgrades in, both tiers are too easy** (12 seeds: Veterano 82%, Campeón 76%). The
  2026-11-20 run (Campeón 49%) was a small sample; easing Campeón (v1) was the wrong direction.
- **The tiers are coupled through the economy.** v2 (Veterano +5%, Campeón +10%) cut loot ~20%, upgrade levels
  by week 24 from 16 to 8, and Campeón fell to 32% on only 34 expeditions: parties reach it poorer and weaker.
- **Kept: v3** (Veterano ×1.18 HP / ×1.13 damage, Campeón ×1.65 / ×1.44; about half of v2's change). Veterano 77%,
  Campeón 65% on 63 expeditions (36 campaigns), deaths 0.15 per expedition (0.30 at Campeón, the edge of the
  band), 34 of 36 campaigns with ≥ 500 ₡ at week 24, 10 upgrade levels by week 24. Every P5-11 band is met.
- **Watch:** Campeón deaths sit at the 0.3 limit, and Aprendiz is 68% (it was 71%): a few more fresh parties
  are hired after Veterano losses. Cursed trinkets are not measured (the bot skips them).
