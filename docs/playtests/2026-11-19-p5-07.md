Also tried on 2 seeds only (48 expeditions, too noisy to judge alone): E2 = E1 + the Mayordomo summons once (35%), E3 = E1 + enemy_dmg 1.05 (25%). Two seeds swing by ±15 points, mostly because a campaign that goes broke early sends ungeared recruits for the rest of its run; settle a change on 6 seeds.

Where the failures come from (E5): all are retreats, never a wipe. The bot calls Retirada when a hero dies in a fight, when two heroes are Al Borde, or when a dragging fight pushes someone to 150 stress (`tools/sim_policy.gd`); about half of the retreats are long boss fights (the Mayordomo with two escorts and two summons runs 10–17 rounds) and half are a death in an early fight.

Veterano and Campeón were not retuned: in 8-week campaigns the bot played 139 of 144 expeditions at Aprendiz (5 at Veterano, too few to judge). Retune them with longer campaigns (`--weeks 24`).
