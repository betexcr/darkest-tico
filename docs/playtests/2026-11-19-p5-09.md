Aprendiz is 0.8/0.8/0.95 throughout (P5-08).

What the long runs showed:

- **Moving up is forced.** A level-3 hero refuses Aprendiz (its range is 0–2), and a hero reaches level 3 after
  about six Aprendiz expeditions. With P5-08's numbers (sized for parties started at the tier with rank 3 and two
  pieces of gear) a party that had just moved up cleared Veterano 30% of the time; failures cost heroes and
  money, the campaign fell back to Aprendiz with fresh recruits, and those went out week after week with stress
  climbing to 200 and no money for the Iglesia.
- **Benching the frayed doesn't save it.** Making the bot keep heroes at 100+ stress home and send free recruits
  instead doubled deaths: level-0 recruits without gear die more than stressed veterans. Reverted.
- **Sizing Veterano and Campeón for the party that arrives fixes the spiral.** First three Veterano expeditions of
  each campaign: 23 of 54 cleared before, 39 of 54 after; 7 of 18 campaigns reached Campeón.
- Aprendiz shows 69% here because it includes Aprendiz runs by rebuilt parties after a failed promotion; fresh
  campaigns clear it 91% (P5-08). Tier-start parties will find Veterano and Campeón easier than these numbers say.
