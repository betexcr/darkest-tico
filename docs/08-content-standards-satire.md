# Content Standards: Satirical Folkloric Tone

The game uses **satire** and **Costa Rican tropes** for humor and identity. This document keeps writing consistent and avoids harmful punch-down humor.

## 1) Goals

- Celebrate recognizable Tico settings (hacienda, buses, cafetal, folklore) without mocking marginalized groups as the joke.
- Horror and comedy share the same rule: **characters have dignity** even when absurd.

## 2) Voice

- Narrator may be arch and melodramatic (Darkest Dungeon lineage).
- Hero barks: short, spoken-aloud lines; mix Spanish with light code-switching only when natural.
- UI copy: clear first, joke second.

## 3) Satire guardrails

- **Punch up or sideways**: institutions, traffic, bureaucracy, coffee snobbery, overwork — fair game.
- **Avoid**: slurs, caricature of Indigenous peoples or Afro-Costa Rican communities, disability as punchline, sexual violence played for laughs.
- **Folklore**: treat legendary figures as **story antagonists** with motive and rules, not as disposable ethnic jokes.

## 4) Stereotype usage

- Stereotypes may appear as **in-world beliefs** (NPCs, pamphlets) that the game can contradict or punish.
- Player-aligned text should not endorse bigotry; villains may embody it for narrative conflict.

## 5) Consistency checklist (per string)

- [ ] Is the joke about behavior or situation, not innate identity?
- [ ] Would this line work if read aloud to a mixed CR audience?
- [ ] Does the enemy or hero still read as a character, not a slur with stats?

## 6) Review

- Flag lines that reference real groups for **cultural review** before ship.
- Keep an alternate **serious** flavor line for sensitive markets if needed.
